Privacy Policy

Last updated 31 October 2026

This policy explains how Barrister A & Associates collects and uses personal information when you visit this website or contact us.

Information we collect

  • Details you send through the consultation form: your name, phone number, email address, type of legal issue, preferred date, description and any document you attach.
  • Basic technical information such as your IP address and browser, used to protect the website from abuse.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, arrange a consultation and, if you instruct us, to act for you. We do not sell or share your information for marketing.

Confidentiality and security

Enquiries and attachments are stored securely and are accessible only to authorised members of our chamber. Information relating to legal matters is treated as confidential.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to instructions are deleted after a reasonable period. Client files are kept for as long as required by law and professional rules.

Your choices

You may ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us using the details on the contact section of this website.
